Court Draft Sim — Privacy Notice

Who we are. Court Draft Sim is a free hobby fan game by Jerdon Philip
Macaraeg, Malasiqui, Pangasinan, PH. Contact jerdonphilipmacaraeg@gmail.com
for privacy requests, corrections, erasure, objections, and takedowns.

What we collect.
- Game state (browser only): lineup, draft pools, minutes, results stored
  as `court-draft-sim-store` in your own browser localStorage. It stays on
  your device except when your browser sends the lineup you chose as part of
  a simulation request you trigger. Clear site data to delete it.
- Server logs (personal data under PH Data Privacy Act RA 10173): IP address,
  date/time, HTTP method/URL/status, user-agent, referrer. URLs may include
  game filters (franchise/decade) if used. We collect NO name, email, account,
  password, payment, precise location, or contacts.

Why + legal basis. Security, debugging, and abuse prevention (rate limiting).
Basis: legitimate interest under DPA Sec. 12(f). No marketing, no profiling,
no sale or sharing for advertising. Where your local law requires consent for
any future non-essential processing, we will ask first.

Recipients / cross-border. Frontend CDN by Vercel (global edge, per Vercel
DPA); API hosting by Render in Singapore; no database today. Fonts are
self-hosted (no Google Fonts transfer). If Postgres (e.g. Neon, Singapore)
or any new processor is added, it will be listed here before use. Source code
may be browsable on GitHub; GitHub's own privacy statement applies if you
visit the repository.

Retention. Raw API logs max 30 days, then deleted or aggregated to anonymous
counts. Browser save persists until you clear it. Backups, if any, roll off
within the same window.

Your rights (DPA Sec. 16 + applicable foreign rights where you reside). Be
informed, object, access, correct, erase/block, claim damages, and complain
to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) or your local authority.
Email above; we reply within a reasonable time. Objecting to security logs
means not using the hosted simulation, because logging is required to run it
securely.

Children. General audience; 13+ recommended, parental guidance below that.
No accounts and no knowing collection beyond technical logs. Parents/guardians
may email for review or erasure.

Changes. Material changes are posted here with a new effective date.
